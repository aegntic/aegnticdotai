# Design Context

## Users

Developers, founders, and technical recruiters who discover aegntic.ai through GitHub, X/Twitter, or direct links. They're evaluating credibility and depth of work. Context: portfolio as proof-of-work — 184 repos, 5,600+ commits, independent builder track record.

## Brand Personality

**Voice**: Quiet confidence. Let the work speak. No hype, no buzzwords.
**Tone**: Restrained precision with editorial boldness.
**3 words**: Precise, Editorial, Independent.
**Emotional goals**: Trust (this person ships real things), curiosity (what else have they built), respect (the craft is evident).

## Aesthetic Direction

**Visual tone**: Hybrid — Apple-like restraint as the foundation, karen.city editorial boldness for moments of emphasis. The whole composition matters more than any individual element. Typography-first design where the grid, whitespace, and type hierarchy do the heavy lifting.

**References**:
- karen.city — editorial layout, bold typographic moments, asymmetric compositions, whitespace as a design tool
- apple.com — restrained precision, confident simplicity, content-first, breathing space

**Anti-references**:
- Generic SaaS landing pages (gradient cards, testimonial sections, feature grids)
- AI-generated aesthetics (cyan-on-dark, glassmorphism, neon accents, sparkline decorations)
- Cluttered layouts — every pixel must earn its place

**Theme**: Dark mode primary. Warm-tinted darks, not cold blue-blacks.

## Design Principles

1. **Composition over decoration** — The overall layout, spacing, and typographic hierarchy create the impression. No decorative elements needed. If removing an element doesn't hurt the page, remove it.

2. **Whitespace is structure** — More whitespace. Let sections breathe. Tight grouping within elements, generous separation between sections. Asymmetric padding creates visual tension.

3. **Typography carries the brand** — A distinctive, readable display font paired with a clean body font. The type scale should have clear steps (at least 1.25 ratio). Never use more than 2 font families.

4. **Zero AI fingerprints** — No cyan-on-dark, no glassmorphism, no gradient text, no sparkline decorations, no rounded rectangles with accent borders, no hero metric layouts. If it looks like AI made it, it's wrong.

5. **Content is the interface** — Real data (184 repos, 5,600+ commits), real projects, real stats. No placeholder content, no generic descriptions. Every word earns its place.
