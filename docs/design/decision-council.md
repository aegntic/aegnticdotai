# Decision: six scenes, one useful route through them

This is a single-model, simulated council exercise. These are reasoning perspectives, not five independent experts or evidence of consensus.

## Independent positions

### Contrarian
The likely 90-day failure is an impressive robot portfolio that never tells a buyer what to buy. Six full-screen stages can double the distance to contact. Keep a persistent enquiry route and make the classification clear in a single sentence per scene. Decorative credibility counters weaken the proposition when the counts are modest or irrelevant.

### First principles
The load-bearing assumption is that visitors understand systems versus agents versus plugins versus products. They often will not. Explain each through its job. Reveal technical detail only after interest. Memory infrastructure and a finished browser utility cannot share the same vague outcome claim.

### Expansionist
A coherent family of machines can become a durable brand asset across product launches, documentation and demos. The compounding value is a reusable art direction and validation process, not six unrelated renders. Preserve source prompts, role, composition, colour and accessible fallbacks.

### Outsider
Editorial museums let an object attract attention, then offer a small label. The website can borrow that rhythm, but cannot hide the exits. Use quiet wordmarks with tactile disclosure and a visible explicit destination. Do not fake customers or imply that every ecosystem project is an established commercial product.

### Executor
Deliver a local six-scene HTML branch first, paired with a genuinely different outcome-first alternative. Test real routes, mobile, motion, focus, failed loading and submission safety. Keep costly continuous video behind a clear budget gate. One accountable implementation owner; a separate evaluator checks it.

## Synthesis

Decision: retain the six-stage journey with distinct machine roles, one-line purpose and selective disclosure; keep enquiry available throughout. Build a second, outcome-first alternative as a true strategic contrast.

Strongest reason: it preserves the visual identity the owner likes while reducing the work needed to understand or contact the practice.

Biggest risk: the taxonomy remains a tour of internal capabilities rather than a buyer's next action. No claim of improved conversion is justified without observations.

Next seven days: implementation owner completes the local scenes and shell; independent evaluator checks accessibility, links and visual coherence; owner compares the two HTML directions; then decide video budget and production release. Do not fabricate partners, traction or a chatbot backend to make the preview feel finished.
