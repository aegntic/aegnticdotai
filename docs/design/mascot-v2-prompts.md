## Systems

```text
Use case: compositing / character consistency.
Asset type: widescreen 3D editorial illustration for the SYSTEMS scene of aegntic.ai.
Input image 1 is the composition/edit target (robots around a mechanical cylinder). Input image 2 is the authoritative mascot identity and BODY-PROPORTION reference, NOT a background reference.
Replace every robot in image 1 with the same compact industrial mascot from image 2, posed to perform the same coordination actions. Preserve the arrangement, number of robots, cylinder, perspective, and large clear upper-right space for live website copy. The leftmost subject may bleed through the left edge; the complete rightmost robot must fit well inside the frame.
Identity lock: the original's oversized squared camera head with a single large black lens on the front, dark graphite body, chrome plate edges, broad substantial torso, very short neck, thick upper arms and forearms, short chunky thighs and shins, heavy block feet. Head approximately one third of the full standing height. Compact mass and unmistakable original silhouette, not lanky or a slender humanoid skeleton. No smiling face, mouth, or cartoon eyes. Sharpen the intricate industrial surfaces into a refined high-resolution render; do not copy compression artifacts from reference 2.
Keep small vivid electric-blue status lights and blue mechanical-core accents, natural black metal and reflective chrome. No teal wash or desaturation.
Backdrop: perfectly flat pure white RGB 255/255/255, seamless to every frame edge, with only small soft localized contact shadows under the mechanism and feet. No gray background, room, horizon, vignette, halo, card, border, typography, logo or watermark.
Output one landscape 1536 by 1024 image. Preserve ample white upper-right space and full mechanical bodies.
```
