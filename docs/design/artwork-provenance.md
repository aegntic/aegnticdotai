## Art direction

Each generated scene shares black graphite, brushed chrome, camera-like optical heads and actual electric-blue lights. Systems coordinate a cylinder; agents inspect a document; plugins fit a cartridge; products offer a finished device; access floats. No text is baked into the art. Left/right negative space is intentional. Current artwork has no edge masks or desaturation filters. An opaque paper background inside each isolated section lets CSS darken blending merge near-white image backgrounds into the page; without that backdrop, a visible white rectangle remained. Desktop Systems art is inset farther from the copy, while mobile labels precede the art vertically. Intentional left bleed remains; the rightmost Systems subject is whole.

Prompts required a very pale warm-white studio background, no dark vignette or bordered panel, purposeful gestures, physically credible materials and empty space for copy. The original artwork's blue accents are retained. These are concept illustrations, not photographs of real hardware or representations of a shipped robot product.
