## Art direction

Each generated scene shares black graphite, brushed chrome, camera-like optical heads and actual electric-blue lights. Systems coordinate a cylinder; agents inspect a document; plugins fit a cartridge; products offer a finished device; access floats. No text is baked into the art. Left/right negative space is intentional. Seam softening is CSS masking outside the primary subjects, not a desaturation filter.

Prompts required a very pale warm-white studio background, no dark vignette or bordered panel, purposeful gestures, physically credible materials and empty space for copy. The original artwork's blue accents are retained. These are concept illustrations, not photographs of real hardware or representations of a shipped robot product.
