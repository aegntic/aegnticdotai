Change this bit. Keep the rest.

Image editing needs an explicit list of what stays unchanged.

Field NotesRetrospective

Retrospective reconstruction of the week beginning 2025-03-31, written on 16 November 2026. This is not a claim of publication at the time.

OpenAI introduced GPT-4o image generation on 25 March, the same day Google announced Gemini 2.5. Easier visual iteration also makes it easier to generate an attractive version of the wrong thing.

An edit has two parts: what changes and what stays. Change the background, keep the face. Adjust the lighting, keep the geometry. Make the lettering easier to read without redesigning the mark.

Write both parts down. If an established logo is being placed into a scene, treat its shape as fixed. Do not ask the generator to reinterpret it and then try to repair the identity afterwards.

Check the result where it will be used. A header needs room for text at narrow widths. A profile image needs to survive a circular crop. A transparent cutout needs clean edges on light and dark backgrounds.

Compare against the original before deciding whether the new version looks better. An improved surface can distract from a changed letter or an altered proportion. Keep the original nearby and reject the edit if it breaks a fixed requirement. There will be other variations.
