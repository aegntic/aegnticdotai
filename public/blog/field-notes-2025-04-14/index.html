Start with the sitemap

April planning asked how the AEGNTIC site should fit together.

Field NotesRetrospective

Retrospective reconstruction of the week beginning 2025-04-14, written on 16 November 2026. This is not a claim of publication at the time.

On 13 April, I asked for a sitemap for AEGNTIC and some loose concepts. That is the recorded starting point here: planning the site, not announcing a finished one.

Google had introduced Agent2Agent on 9 April. Software coordination was getting its own protocol conversation. For the website, the immediate problem was simpler. What does someone need to find, and where do they go next?

Give each page a job. Let research explain an idea, let a product page show what can be used, and let the blog carry the decisions behind it. If everything repeats the same introduction, adding pages has not added much.

Mark the boundaries accurately too. A link to another platform opens that platform. An export gives someone a file. Neither should imply automatic synchronisation or a completed connection that has not been built.

The sitemap is a cheap place to discover a dead end. Follow a visitor’s route from the first question to an actual result before spending days on transitions between sections. A page with no clear purpose can be removed while it is still just a box in the plan.
