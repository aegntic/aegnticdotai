Flat and lifeless is still flat and lifeless

The personal site needed a story and better assets, not more technical copy.

Field NotesRetrospective

Retrospective field note for 2026-09-28, reconstructed on 16 November 2026. The archive date is not a claim of publication at the time.

On 25 September, my feedback on the personal site was that it looked flat and lifeless. I supplied hero and logo direction and asked for creative storytelling and intrigue. The technical detail could live at aegntic.ai.

That division matters. A visitor should not have to read a stack inventory before getting a sense of the person and the work. Give the opening a point of view. Choose the image, pace the reveal and make the next action clear.

The follow-up was less abstract: the hero images were blurry and the logo needed to be the correct AEGNTIC mark. Those details can undo an ambitious composition. Open the image at the size it will render. Check the edge quality. Use the actual identity asset.

This records a critique and a direction, not proof that the final site achieved it. More generated sections would not answer the brief. The page needed stronger choices about what to show, with enough restraint to let the good material carry the experience.
