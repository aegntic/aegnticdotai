Be creative. Keep the essence.

The earlier two-hand logo brief remains a constraint on new image tools.

Field NotesRetrospective

Retrospective field note for 2026-11-09, reconstructed on 16 November 2026. The archive date is not a claim of publication at the time.

On 28 July, the logo instruction was to use both hands, be creative and not lose the essence. That earlier brief carries forward into November’s image-tool context. It records exploration, not a delivered identity system.

Keep the defining idea visible while changing the presentation. Framing, texture and balance can move around. If the two-hand concept disappears under the treatment, the variation has wandered away from the brief.

Test the mark without the impressive mockup. Small size. One colour. Header placement. An image that looks good across a full screen may fail as an actual logo.

Use an appropriate production format and preserve the original reference. Otherwise each round gets compared with the previous variation, and the original idea slowly drops out of view.

Images 2.6 adds another way to explore. Selection still requires taste. Choose the changes that strengthen the mark, discard the ones that merely make it busier, and hand over an asset that can survive the places it is supposed to be used.

OpenAI introduced Images 2.6 on 4 November. Source.
