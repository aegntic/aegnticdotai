Where did that answer come from?

Deep research made multi-step investigation more visible. Its claims still need checking.

Field NotesRetrospective

Retrospective reconstruction of the week beginning 2025-02-03, written on 16 November 2026. This is not a claim of publication at the time.

OpenAI released o3-mini on 31 January and introduced deep research on 2 February. Research was becoming a more explicit workflow: go out, gather material and bring back a synthesis.

The first check is whether the answer addresses the actual decision. If the question is which tool supports an existing setup, a long history of the market is probably in the way. Find the compatibility details, costs and restrictions that could change the choice.

Then open the sources. Several articles repeating one announcement are still one underlying claim. A vendor launch page tells you what was announced. It does not establish how the product performed in someone else’s workload.

Dates need the same attention. A page updated months later may describe features unavailable when the original announcement appeared. This archive keeps those dates separate for that reason.

Keep the conclusion short enough to use, with the decisive evidence beside it. If one missing fact prevents a recommendation, name that fact. There is no benefit in burying it under another page of confident prose.
