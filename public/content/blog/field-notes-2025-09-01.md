> Retrospective field note for 2025-09-01. Reconstructed in November 2026 from dated sources, not originally published on the archive date.

On 1 September, I asked for LLM.deals as a directory: Angular, automatic updates, a news feed and blog creation. That was the brief. It wasn't a claim that all of it already worked.

The easy version is a grid of tools with nice cards. The harder version saves someone from opening twenty tabs and still not knowing which option fits.

DeepSeek-V3.1 had arrived on 21 August. Releases like that expose the maintenance problem immediately. A description can become stale without looking broken. So can pricing, availability and a comparison written with last month's assumptions.

Every listing needs a source and a checked date. Keep the billing unit and conditions attached to a price. If a field can't be verified, show that instead of letting generated copy smooth over the gap.

Automatic updates should produce a record of what changed. They shouldn't silently rewrite a listing because a crawler found a different sentence. Preserve the previous value so a wrong update can be understood and reversed.

The blog has a job here too: explain a change that affects a choice. It doesn't need to manufacture a hands-on review for every release. A smaller directory that answers the question beats a larger one that just reorganises the search results.
