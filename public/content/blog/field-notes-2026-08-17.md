> Retrospective field note for 2026-08-17, reconstructed on 16 November 2026. The archive date is not a claim of publication at the time.

Another model release does not create an obligation to migrate this afternoon.

Name the expected improvement first. Fewer formatting failures? Lower time to a finished result? Better handling of a case the current system repeatedly misses? Put the candidate through that test without switching the customer-facing route.

Include maintenance in the comparison. A slight gain may arrive with three new prompt branches and another special case. A less dramatic improvement may remove an entire repair step. The second change can be worth more to the team maintaining the product.

Keep curiosity experiments separate from commitments. There is nothing wrong with exploring a new model because it looks interesting. It just should not quietly become a production migration with no stated benefit.

Gemini 3.7 Flash provides another option. Save the comparison, adopt it if the result warrants the change, or leave the working system alone. Either outcome should let the team get back to the actual project rather than orbiting the next announcement.
