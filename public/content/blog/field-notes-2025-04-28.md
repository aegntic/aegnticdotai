*Retrospective reconstruction of the week beginning 2025-04-28, written on 16 November 2026. This is not a claim of publication at the time.*

On 22 April, the site discussion brought research, products, a blog and email signup into one concept, with a three-dimensional visual direction. Those were ideas being explored. The chat does not prove they were deployed.

There is a sensible route through those pieces. Read about a problem, inspect the work behind it, try something relevant and decide whether to follow its development. Each step should answer something the previous step raised.

March's agent-building tools and April's A2A announcement were part of the surrounding AI discussion. Connected systems were interesting. The site still needed to make its own connections understandable to a person arriving cold.

A strong visual identity can help. It can also bury the route under a scene that takes too long to load or leaves the controls hard to read. Try the narrow screen early. Keep headings legible and buttons recognisable.

The email signup should explain what someone will receive. Using a tool should not quietly subscribe them to something else. There is enough going on in the concept already; the visitor should be able to choose one useful thing and do it without deciphering the whole site.
