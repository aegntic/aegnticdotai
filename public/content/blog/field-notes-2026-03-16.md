> Retrospective field note for 2026-03-16, reconstructed on 16 November 2026. The archive date is not a claim of publication at the time.

A beautiful five-second clip can be useless in the sequence. The character changes, the movement has nowhere to go, or the next shot starts from a completely different visual world.

Sketch the edit first. Duration, framing, transitions, the job of each shot. A rough storyboard can reveal the missing connection before another batch of generation.

Keep exact material out of the uncertain part. Product labels, interface text and legal copy can be composited separately. There is little value in regenerating an entire scene because the lettering is wrong again.

Watch the sequence where it will be seen. On a phone, with the sound off. Are the captions readable? Does the cut arrive before the viewer has understood the image? Does compression erase the texture that made the shot interesting?

Sora's API expansion increases the production options. Selection still matters. Keep notes on rejected takes so the next attempt fixes the right problem. A weak concept and an unreliable render need different responses. More footage will not necessarily solve either one.
