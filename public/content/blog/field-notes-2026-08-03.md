> Retrospective field note for 2026-08-03, reconstructed on 16 November 2026. The archive date is not a claim of publication at the time.

A plan based on a still image can be obsolete before the movement starts. Someone reaches into the scene. An object shifts. The system needs to notice and stop.

Keep the observation, proposed action and execution separate enough to inspect. Record which assumptions the action depends on. A convincing description of the scene does not establish that those assumptions remain true.

Controlled demonstrations are valuable within their limits. Known objects and a cleared workspace are very different from a busy room with fragile items and people moving unpredictably. Describe the tested environment without quietly extending the claim to everything outside it.

The stop mechanism needs to be immediate and dependable. It cannot rely on an operator catching a subtle misunderstanding halfway through a spoken explanation. Clarification belongs before an irreversible action.

Robotics ER 2 is a development to investigate. Any application built around such capability still needs evidence at the action boundary: what was observed, what was authorised and what confirms the next movement is safe.
