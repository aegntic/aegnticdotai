*Retrospective reconstruction of the week beginning 2025-05-05, written on 16 November 2026. This is not a claim of publication at the time.*

On 29 April, I asked for an actionable task list an agent could navigate, including pre-emptive error handling. It was for an AI-video workflow. Where checkout needed me, I wanted the page already prepared, then the workflow able to continue after sign-in.

That was a requested setup, not a completed system. The distinction matters because a detailed plan can sound remarkably like something that already works.

April's A2A announcement was about connecting agents. This request exposed another connection worth designing properly: the point where automated preparation reaches a human decision.

Do the available work first. Gather the requirements, navigate to the right place and explain the actual commitment. After the person acts, check the result before continuing. A signed-in browser does not prove a purchase succeeded.

If the page times out, inspect the transaction state. Do not repeat it just because the confirmation was missed. If the session expires, preserve the completed work.

The handoff should be small enough to deal with and clear enough to trust. Sending another setup guide at that point would put the work straight back on the person who delegated it.
