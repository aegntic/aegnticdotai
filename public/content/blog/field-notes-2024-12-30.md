*Retrospective reconstruction of the week beginning 2024-12-30, written on 16 November 2026. This is not a claim of publication at the time.*

DeepSeek released V3 on 26 December. More options to build with. Good. Now pick something worth building.

Take a catalogue with inconsistent product descriptions. Can the model fix the formatting without changing dimensions, inventing materials or dropping a warning? That is a small job, but it has a clear result. You can put the original beside the output and check it.

Start there. Keep ten messy examples, including a few that need a question rather than a confident rewrite. Run the same examples through the models being considered. Count the corrections. Keep the failed outputs too, because those tell you where the work is going to land when real people use it.

Open weights also leave hosting and maintenance decisions in your hands. That can be useful freedom. It can also turn a simple feature into another server to look after. Downloading the model does not finish the setup.

Before adding a chat box, show the task working. One clean result from an awkward input is a better starting point than a homepage promising to transform everything.
