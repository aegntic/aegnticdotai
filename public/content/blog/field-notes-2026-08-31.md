> Retrospective field note for 2026-08-31, reconstructed on 16 November 2026. The archive date is not a claim of publication at the time.

On 27 August, I asked a hypothetical: could a workflow record my own screen and audio, categorise moments by product, and help turn them into scripts and updates for YouTube and X?

The archive records the idea. It does not establish a working capture system or a published series.

A sensible experiment would start with one selected session. Exclude private windows and credentials. Decide what gets retained before recording a large archive. Other people's conversations do not become publishable because they happen to appear during the work.

Then inspect what the system selects. A breakthrough, a discarded approach and a finished artifact are different moments. The surrounding explanation matters. A casual idea should not become a triumphant product story merely because it makes a good clip.

The handoff could be quite small: selected footage, timestamps and an editable draft structure. Review those before automating more. Multimodal input gives the workflow material to work with; deciding what deserves an audience is still an editorial task.
