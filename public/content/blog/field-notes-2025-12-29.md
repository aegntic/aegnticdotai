> Retrospective field note for 2025-12-29. Reconstructed in November 2026 from dated sources, not originally published on the archive date.

The year's announcements are easy to list. The project history is less tidy, which makes it more interesting.

April has the aegntic.ai sitemap exploration. May has a research-to-project workflow and a demand for actual design examples. June has the mission and quality-language brief. July has the portfolio that shouldn't depend on VisualMind surviving. August has local model questions. September has LLM.deals. November has the attempt to make the wider project map coherent.

Those are documented directions and decisions. They aren't proof that every proposed feature shipped. I'm not turning the gaps into a success story because the archive would read more smoothly that way.

GPT-5.2's December release closes the public-source trail for this entry. The year brought more ways to generate, code, research and act. Across the chats, the more specific concern was what happened around those capabilities: how the result was packaged, whether it could be inspected, and how much work still landed back on the person who asked.

That's the history worth keeping. Which decision made the next task easier? Which impressive idea remained an idea? Which dependency was worth avoiding?

Leave the unanswered questions in the record. They give the next year something real to work on.
