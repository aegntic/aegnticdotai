*Retrospective reconstruction of the week beginning 2025-04-07, written on 16 November 2026. This is not a claim of publication at the time.*

GPT-4o's image-generation release made another round of variations easy to request. Before requesting more, shrink the one already on screen.

A detailed mark can look excellent at full size and disappear in a navigation bar. Fine highlights merge. Small lettering becomes texture. The feature that made the image interesting may be the first thing lost in a social thumbnail.

Put it in the actual layout. Use the intended background. Crop the hero at the narrowest supported width. View the thumbnail without zooming in to rescue it. If the subject is unclear there, adding more detail is unlikely to help.

Make two separate passes. First, check the required content: spelling, shape, subject and crop. Then decide whether the styling works. It is easier to notice an incorrect letter before being distracted by the finish.

A small set of honest previews can end the variation loop faster than another ambitious prompt. Keep the version that works in the awkward placement as well as the flattering one. That is where people will actually encounter it.
