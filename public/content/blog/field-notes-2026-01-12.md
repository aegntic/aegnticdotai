> Retrospective field note for 2026-01-12, reconstructed on 16 November 2026. The archive date is not a claim of publication at the time.

Change the background, and somehow the label changes too. Better lighting, slightly different garment. A beautiful result that can no longer be used.

That is the annoying part of generative editing: the requested change gets all the attention, while the rest of the image quietly drifts. Put the original beside the output. Check the logo, lettering, seams, silhouette and anything else that identifies the subject. Zoom in, then look again at the size it will actually be displayed.

A brief should say what can move and what must stay exact. The untouched area is part of the job. It does not get a free pass because the new background looks impressive.

And use the straightforward tool when it fits. A mask, crop or colour adjustment can be faster and more exact than regenerating the scene. Save invention for the part that needs it. There is no prize for making a simple retouch harder to control.
