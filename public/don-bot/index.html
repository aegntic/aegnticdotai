PUBLIC ARCHITECTURAL BLUEPRINT

Built to run for hours while you stay near the loop.

In the loop, you prompt and babysit. Near the loop, a small durable team debates, builds, and verifies before escalating only high-leverage decisions to your inbox.

1 Lead Orchestrator + 4 Durable Specialists

Instead of spawning 50 ephemeral subagents that burn tokens and overwrite each other's files, Don-Bot runs a persistent 5-agent roster over SQLite message rooms. Before writing code, the team conducts a 3-round architectural negotiation and autonomously tunes its own Core 4 parameters.

  • Social Contract Before Code: Specialists agree on contracts, schemas, and file boundaries in a room before taking a single-writer lease.
  • Autonomous Core 4 Self-Tuning: Each specialist dynamically downshifts or upgrades its Model, Thinking Effort, Context Window, and Skillset per milestone.
  • Bounded Subagent Fan-Out: Specialists can spawn up to 3 parallel workers with a strict $0.15 cost cap for parallel research or verification.
