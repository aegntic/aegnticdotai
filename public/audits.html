Questions

Is this just an AI prompt?

No. The prompt architecture is the engine; the value is in the brief normalization, the deduplication, the adversarial collision, and the verdict contract. You are paying for a decision review, not a chatbot output.

Why fifteen advisers and not five?

Five is enough for thin briefs (Mode 2). Decisions with multiple tensions get the full fifteen (Mode 3). The system deduplicates aggressively. If two advisers converge on the same argument, the weaker one drops or is forced to add a distinct angle.

Do you actually use named operators like Buffett and Musk?

As compressed reasoning lenses, not impersonations. Each named tag applies a distinct strategic heuristic, prioritization logic, and failure model. No catchphrases, no public-speaking tics, no caricature.

What if my decision is too sensitive to share?

Redact what you must. The brief schema accepts placeholders for confidential figures. We can also sign an NDA for Live Council and Retainer engagements.

When should I not use this?

Trivial decisions, purely factual lookups, or anything where you already know the answer and want validation. The council is for expensive calls under genuine uncertainty.

Have a decision worth pressure-testing?

Send a one-sentence brief. We will tell you whether it warrants a full council, a reduced council, or a direct answer. No obligation.
